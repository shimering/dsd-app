# Smile Studio v2 review workflow

Development branch: `rebuild-v2`. Production branch: `main`. Cloudflare automatically deploys pushes to `main` to the existing `dsd-app` worker. Pushing only to `rebuild-v2` does not update the hosted app. The tooth controls, proportions, and lighting release was published on 29 September 2026; see [TEETH_CONTROLS.md](TEETH_CONTROLS.md).

1. **Photos:** upload JPEG, PNG, or WebP, or capture using the device's camera picker. Add multiple views, retain the original bytes, and change rotation without resampling. Photos and generated previews remain in IndexedDB. Case records show visible local save failures.
2. **Measure:** fit six adjustable smile-frame guides across FDI 15–25 before outlining the lips: midline, smile curve, interdental proportion, central incisor proportion, gingival curve, and papilla curve. Proportions are visual design targets; hidden landmarks retain their identities. Confirm guides before displaying them in Teeth. Gemini proposes six tool placements for review while preserving configured targets and confirmed guides. See [DSD_MEASUREMENTS.md](DSD_MEASUREMENTS.md). Free distances, polylines, angles (second point is the vertex), guide lines, and freehand annotations remain available. Calibrate with a clinician-confirmed reference in the measurement plane. Uncalibrated lengths use pixels; calibrated lengths are projected millimeters. Select a point to adjust its original-photo coordinates. Cancel a gesture to discard it; undo/redo restores geometry while advancing the revision.
3. **Lip outline:** trace the inner upper border from one corner to the other, then return along the inner lower border. Move points, insert after a selected point, remove points, and tune smoothing. Only a confirmed simple outline clips the mockup. Editing a point reopens the draft.
4. **Teeth:** place all ten upper teeth, FDI 15,14,13,12,11,21,22,23,24,25 from image left to right. Source tooth PNGs were generated with ImageGen and revised to give every tooth a rounded convex cervical contour and both premolars one visible buccal cusp. Atlas gutters are removed in the runtime renderer, so placement uses each actual crown's bounds. Central incisors share a baseline; lateral incisors are shorter; posterior teeth rise on a symmetric provisional smile curve. Individual or whole-smile adjustments remain clinician-controlled. Four forms, three textures, and five visual shades are available. Duplicate alternatives and save reusable presets.
5. **Compare:** both sides use the identical source image and viewport. Editing guides are hidden. Mockup and optional AI preview affect only the confirmed lip opening. Export a simulation or comparison PNG with a simulation label.

Open **Tooth library** to inspect the corrected ten-tooth arch before applying it to a photo. Initial placement is a provisional template, not an anatomical detection.

After placing the overlays, use **Recalculate from measurements** in Teeth to fit the active design to confirmed guides. It applies the ten-tooth ruler, central width/height target, dental midline, and visible smile/gingival curves. Draft or unavailable guides are ignored. Styles, visibility, perspective, lighting, and other alternatives are preserved; the whole recalculation is one undo step. Changing a guide alone still leaves tooth layers unchanged. See [TEETH_CONTROLS.md](TEETH_CONTROLS.md) for fitting priorities when the guides disagree.

In **Teeth**, select a tooth, then pinch with two fingers to scale and twist to rotate. Enable **Move and style the whole smile** to transform the arch together. **Apply snapping** attracts nearby 5° angles and 5% scale steps; turn it off for continuous movement. Use **Pan** to navigate the photo with two fingers. The other stages retain two-finger pan/zoom. Enable finger edit mode for single-finger placement and editing. The magnifier shows the original photo around a selected location. Desktop browser automation does not establish physical iPad/Pencil support.

**Lighting & shadows** adjusts the whole active design: brightness, warmth, saturation, highlight softness, upper-lip shadow and its reach, posterior shadow, and left/right lighting balance. **Match photo** estimates starting values from the original enamel inside the confirmed lip opening; refine the sliders manually. Settings are saved with alternatives, presets, and backups and appear in simulation/comparison exports. See [TEETH_CONTROLS.md](TEETH_CONTROLS.md) for use and release notes.

Starting tooth heights are based on crown width, so a deep lip opening cannot elongate the teeth. **Restore natural proportions** repairs the selected tooth or whole smile while keeping its cervical midpoint in place. The proportions remain adjustable per case and are preserved during pinch scaling.

## Accounts, backup, and AI

Existing Supabase accounts are reused. Only new `dsd_workspaces` structured records sync. Owner RLS, column permissions, and optimistic revisions prevent cross-account access or silent overwrites. Conflicting cloud records load into a separate local copy. Local guest cases and each account's cases have separate scopes.

Cloud revisions now persist on this device across refreshes and sign-ins. Gemini automatically checks for an existing case when its revision is unknown, reconnecting identical records without creating a duplicate. If the local and cloud records differ, **Choose a case version** offers **Continue with my local edits** or **Use the cloud version**. Both choices preserve the local edits as a separate case and retain the original cloud case. After choosing, request Gemini assistance again. Loading cloud cases also saves their revisions, and late responses after an account change cannot enter another account's workspace.

Export a JSON backup before moving devices. Backups include original and generated bytes, calibration, geometry, designs, and presets. Imports create new cases and revoke AI consent; original dimensions and SHA-256 fingerprints are validated. Reattach missing originals only when dimensions and fingerprint match. Media is not uploaded to Supabase Storage.

AI is optional and requires a signed-in account and recorded patient consent before any photo is sent. `smile-assist` checks ownership, consent, current photo revision, and a 15-second request interval before calling Gemini. Coordinates are normalized then accepted into the original-image coordinate system; lengths and angles are calculated locally. Proposed endpoints, outline points, and tooth placements are previewed before application. Intersecting outlines, duplicate tooth identifiers, assumed scales, invalid images, and stale proposals are rejected. Rendered previews retain editable tooth layers and are clipped to the original lip opening; changed aspect ratios are rejected.

Defaults are `gemini-3.5-flash` for assistance and `gemini-3.1-flash-image` for rendering. The user selected 3.5 after the previous assistance model/request was rejected by the provider. Models can be configured; there is no silent fallback or automatic retry. Provider credentials remain in server secrets. Assistance uses `generateContent` JSON mode, with the expected schema in the prompt and full server validation of the returned object. Live checks found that the account accepted this outline format while rejecting provider-enforced schemas. Rendering uses the Gemini Interactions API with `store:false`; normal provider processing policies still apply. A temporary provider-overload error asks you to retry shortly. Account/provider outages leave manual tools available.

## Review gates and roadmap

Review the manual editor and tooth assets on real photos before expanding scope. Physical iPad Safari and Apple Pencil testing, authenticated end-to-end Gemini testing, and clinician review remain required acceptance checks.

Keep gingivectomy, periodontal assessments, consultation, treatment reports, lower-arch design, and 3D deferred until that review. The earlier planning and research remain reference material, not a validation of this prototype.
